HermesAssistant Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Overview

HermesAssistant is a private, single-user personal assistant application. It is operated for personal use and is not a public or commercial service.

Information Accessed

HermesAssistant may access information from Google services that the application has been explicitly authorized to access, including Google Calendar and Google Tasks.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed from Google services is used solely to provide personal assistant functionality, such as viewing calendar events, managing tasks, and supporting personal scheduling.

Information Sharing

HermesAssistant does not sell personal information or share Google user data with third parties for advertising or other unrelated purposes.

Data Storage

Access credentials and application data are stored for the operation of the private assistant and are protected using the security controls available in the application's environment.

Data Retention

Information is retained only as necessary for the operation of the personal assistant and may be deleted by its owner.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the owner of HermesAssistant through the email address associated with the application.
